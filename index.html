Executive Hangar Timer FAQ

Quick answers about the Pyro Executive Hangar, LED cycle, compboards, and contested-zone support timers.

What does this tracker show?

It follows the Executive Hangar cycle in Pyro, showing whether the hangar is closed, opening soon, ready for compboard insertion, or in the death zone.

When is the Executive Hangar open?

The hangar becomes accessible once all five LEDs are green, and stays accessible until the LEDs finish powering down.

How does the 5 LED cycle work?

The red phase charges the LEDs one by one. Once all five are green, the access window opens; the green phase then counts the LEDs back down.

What are the manual support timers for?

They help coordinate contested-zone runs — terminals, tablets, keycards, and compboard routes around the hangar loop.
